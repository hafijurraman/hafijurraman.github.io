An American Sign Language (ASL) classifier built by fine-tuning Hugging Face's pretrained Vision Transformer (ViT), using data augmentation and class weighting to enhance robustness.

The model achieves an overall accuracy of 94% on a diverse dataset of over 26,000 images, demonstrating strong practical applicability for visual recognition tasks.

**Tech:** PyTorch, Hugging Face Transformers, Vision Transformer (ViT).

Code: [github.com/hafijurraman/asl-vit](https://github.com/hafijurraman/asl-vit)
